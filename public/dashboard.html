    About Streetwise

    Streetwise was created to make IT education more engaging and practical. Instead of only reading about technology, students can learn by working through exercises, simulations, and realistic IT challenges. The website was created as part of the Congressional App Challenge, a nationwide competition for students to create apps and websites.
