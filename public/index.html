About Streetwise

Streetwise was created to make IT education more engaging and practical. Instead of only reading about technology, students can now learn by working through exercises, simulations, and realistic IT challenges. The website was created as part of the Congressional App Challenge, a nationwide competition for students to create apps and websites. Due to the background of the creators, we decided to focus the project on IT education while making the website as interactive and practical as possible.
